Frequently asked questions

  • How do I enable the keyboard on iPhone? Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards → Add New Keyboard → YouVyron.
  • Why does it ask for Full Access? The AI features (fix, translate, reply) need the internet to send the text you choose to our server. Typing and suggestions work without Full Access, and we do not collect what you type.
  • The keyboard disappears in password fields? That is iOS itself, for every third-party keyboard; it comes back once you leave the field.
  • How do I delete my data? Email us from the same address and we delete your device's data within 30 days, as described in the privacy policy.
